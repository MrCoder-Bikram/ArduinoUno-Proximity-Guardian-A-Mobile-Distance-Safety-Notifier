Abstract:
We are working on a project to solve a major issue of getting eye defects due to watching mobile
phones too close to the eye. Designing an IoT-based Smart Distance Monitoring System. This
system will use sensors to measure how close to the screen the user is with his face.On new
mobile phones, if the screen is held too close to your eye, the device will alert the user. Reasons
to work on this project:
1. Cause:
Mobile phones are used too frequently by young and old alike, posing a serious risk to vision
which is a major concern among people. Many people tend to hold their because people often
hold phones too close to their eyes while studying, watching videos, etc. Whether hands-free,
Bluetooth or just normal talking - as well as texting. For a long time we have being exposed to a
very short distance to the brain. The recommended safe viewing distance of around 25–30 cm.
Such habits put constant pressure on the eye – in short, they can lead to vision problems.
2. Effect:
Humans are constantly being exposed to EMF and RF. In recent years, there has been growing
controversy and debate as to whether this could have severe effects on the human body such as
eye fatigue and vision disorders like myopia(nearsightedness). Myopia occurs when the eyeball
elongates appear hazy or indistinct, this eyesight problem can gradually affect and the vision
may appear blurry. Symptoms may be accompanied by eye irritation, headache, eye dryness and
with a blurred vision. Over time, this condition can become more prominent, significantly
impacting academic performance of students and professional performance of the adults,
especially among people heavily dependent on digital devices.
3. Solution:
To mitigate these issues, we are designing an IoT-based Smart Irrigation System. This is a
Distance Monitoring System uses sensors to monitor the distances of particular objects. It will
detect how close your face is to the phone. Then the unit will sound an alarm to keep some space
in between. This automated feedback mechanism promotes proper screen usage habits and
effectively reduces the risk of myopia and digital eye strain.
